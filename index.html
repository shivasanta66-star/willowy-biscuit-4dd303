About the showroom

Maa Bidri Motors is the Honda showroom on SH 39 in Dhodra. Riders come to us from Dhodra, Umerkote, Papadahandi, Jharigam and the villages around.

Come in with your budget and your questions. We will go through the on-road price, EMI options and what your old two-wheeler is worth, take you for a test ride if you want one, and get the insurance and registration done before you ride home. After that, bring the bike back to us for servicing.
