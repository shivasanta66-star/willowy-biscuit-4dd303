Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 August 2026

The short version

This website does not collect anything about you. It has no accounts, no analytics, and no server of its own. Nothing you type into the enquiry form is sent to us by the website itself. The one exception is the embedded Google Map on the location section, which loads from Google and is covered below.

What happens when you use the enquiry form

When you press Send Enquiry on WhatsApp, the page opens WhatsApp on your device with a message already written out, addressed to the showroom. Nothing leaves your device until you press send inside WhatsApp. If you close WhatsApp without sending, we never receive anything, and no copy is kept anywhere.

The message contains only what you filled in: your name, your mobile number, what you are interested in, how you prefer to be contacted, and your note. Once you send it, that message sits in the showroom's WhatsApp like any other chat.

What we do with your details

We use your name and mobile number for one thing: replying to your enquiry and, where you have asked for it, arranging a test ride, a service visit, or a price quotation. We do not sell your details, share them with advertisers, or add you to marketing lists without asking you first.

Where a finance or insurance application needs your details to be passed to a bank or insurer, we will tell you at the time and ask before doing it.

Calls and WhatsApp

Calling or messaging the showroom shares your phone number with us, as any call or message does. WhatsApp itself is run by Meta, not by us, and their handling of your data is covered by WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

Google services on this site

The location section contains a Google Maps frame that loads with the page. This means that when you open our site, your browser contacts Google's servers directly, and Google receives your IP address and may set its own cookies, exactly as if you had visited Google Maps yourself. That is Google's processing under Google's privacy policy, not ours — we receive nothing from it.

The Get Directions buttons also open Google Maps. Once you are there you are on Google's service, under their terms.

The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which is another request to Google's servers carrying your IP address, as any request to any website does.

Keeping and removing your information

Enquiries live in the showroom's WhatsApp and in our own records of who we have sold to or serviced for. If you would like us to delete your enquiry, call or message +91 99376 01505 and we will remove it.

Children

This site is meant for adults buying and servicing two-wheelers, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If how we handle enquiries changes, we will update this page and change the date above.
